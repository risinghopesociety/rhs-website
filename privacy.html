Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Rising Hope Society (RHS) is a community welfare organisation in Khairpur Tamewali, Bahawalpur. This page explains what personal information we collect on this website, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

What we collect

We only collect what you type into our forms.

  • Membership registration: full name, father or husband name, CNIC, date of birth, gender, mobile number, email, address, province, profession, membership type and a passport-size photo.
  • Help requests: the same identity and contact details, plus the kind of help you need and the amount required.
  • Contact form: your name, email and message.

Why we collect it

  • To register members and issue membership cards and certificates.
  • To review help requests and contact the people who applied.
  • To let a member verify their certificate and see their own donation record using their CNIC and date of birth.
  • To reply to your messages and send receipts for donations.

What we show publicly

The homepage shows only totals, such as the number of active members and approved cases. We do not publish your CNIC, phone number, address or photo on public pages.

Where your data is stored

Your information is stored with Google Firebase (database) and Cloudinary (uploaded photos and images). These services process data on our behalf. Only authorised RHS administrators can open the admin panel.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only when the law requires it or when it is needed to deliver help to you.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time. Write to risinghopesociety@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp, and tell us the CNIC you registered with.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
